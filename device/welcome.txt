Welcome to ESP32 Shell!

Try: help, ls -l, pwd, free, df, uname, ifconfig

Your files live in /home and survive a reboot.
Example:
  echo "hello ESP32" > hello.txt
  cat hello.txt
  python -c "print(2 + 3)"

This is MicroPython, not Linux. No bash, apt, native Linux binaries,
pipes, background jobs or Unix user permissions.
